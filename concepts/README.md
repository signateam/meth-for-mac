# Meth vs Amphetamine comparison concepts

Five square, 1200 × 1200 comparison images:

1. `concept-1.png`: direct headline and side-by-side cards. Recommended starting point for a social post.
2. `concept-2.png`: Meth's three-mode menu beside Amphetamine's broader capability set.
3. `concept-3.png`: three-step Meth path, with Amphetamine's customization acknowledged.
4. `concept-4.png`: editorial comparison table for readers evaluating both apps.
5. `concept-5.png`: closed-laptop product poster with a compact comparison.

`index.html` is the editable source. Open `index.html?n=1` through `n=5` to see individual concepts.

## Source and claim notes

- Meth's icon comes from this repository's `dist/icon-1024.png`; its modes and one-time setup are documented in this repository's [README](../README.md).
- Amphetamine's icon comes from its [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amphetamine/id937984704?mt=12). That listing describes manual sessions, timed sessions, Triggers, closed-display behavior, display controls, custom icons, and hot keys.
- The “under 10 seconds” timing was supplied in the creative brief. These concepts scope it to turning on Meth's closed-lid mode **after one-time setup**. It has not been independently benchmarked here.
- Amphetamine's listing also describes a simple manual on/off action. No measured Amphetamine setup time was found, so none of the images assigns it a number or claims its basic action is slower.

The images are visual drafts for review, not published ads.
